# v1.0 Encounters — the Ironwood leg

Content design for the slice. Tick data lives in TUNING_V0.md; naming in DECISIONS.md D6. Lore authority: `docs/lore/CONSOLIDATED_LORE_SURFACE.md`, `CAST_BIBLE.md`. Canon-locked beats are marked ⚓.

## Beat sheet (play order)
1. **Cabin prologue (~8 min)** — interior teaches move / interact / Attend through three care interactions: Water (draw and carry), Bread (break and share with Iiro), and **dosing Anna — which IS the flask tutorial**. The doses the player prepares are the doses they will inherit. No HUD until the first dose; the border ornament "wakes" with it.
2. **Doorway 1v1** — wolves take the door; forced-corridor first fight with one wolf. Teaches dodge commitment, Attend optional, loot-as-consequence.
3. **Yard 3-wolf** — woodpile chokepoint; teaches positioning + the pack token (only one wolf commits at a time).
4. **Forest + shortcuts** — two loops back toward the cabin: one drop-down (teaches jump/fall), one unlockable gate. Road ambush of 4 with **Iiro's scripted flight route** (timer-paced, soft failure only: wounded → later state change; never a babysat escort AI). ⚓ Objective is Iiro's safety, not kill count (ADR 0008 spirit).
5. **Anna's death** ⚓ — return to the cabin. Staged-camera, non-combat presence-verb scene (ObserveBreath, SitNear, HoldHand, SpeakName, Pray, KeepWatch, WitnessDeath, WriteName). Fixed outcome: "Anna's breath stops with Kalev still beside her." Hearth dims ~18%. Her remaining doses become the vial — **borrowed mercy**, stated in one notebook line, not a tutorial popup.
6. **Taking the hearth iron** — Kalev takes the fire iron from Anna's hearth as the opening weapon (material memory: a warming tool turned weapon).
7. **The road** — ambient Wither off-path (0.5/s); first Hearth beyond the cabin; Names banking taught by writing.
8. **The Warden of the Ironwood** — boss, below.
9. **Birdie coda** ⚓ — post-boss road threshold: the apple refusal (mandatory, non-combat, cheap, load-bearing). She misnames Kalev "Caleb." Road bends toward Bethany. Slice ends.

## Wolf pack grammar
Real animals — no glowing eyes, no fantasy scale, zero Wither application, lootable as consequence (hide/sinew/tooth/meat). Three roles:
- **Baiter** — circles, feints, low commitment; exists to spend your attention.
- **Lunger** — long-telegraph pounce (30t), the punishable one.
- **Harrier** — fast flank bite (16t), the one that punishes tunnel vision.
Hard rule: one attack token per 3.5m ring; token releases 45t after resolution; others circle/reposition. A howl escalates the pack by one. Leash 25m. Under 25% Pulse a wolf flees and returns ~40s later. Encounter ladder: doorway 1v1 → yard 3 → road 4 with route timer → optional deep-wood den (loot, Wither pressure).

## The Warden of the Ironwood
**Who** ⚓-adjacent (council-invented, canon-extended): a Continuance forest warden who kept his line after the asking died. The Turn as a person: perfect procedure, no why. Speech is role-fragments only: "Line's mine." / "Off the line." / "Tagged." He cannot be talked down — the tragedy is that there is no one left to talk to. Halloway rule: wounded, plausible, partly right — never zombie-horror. Polyphonic identity: folk "the keeper on the road"; State "Ironwood Warden, Continuance post 9"; Church "the lamp that stayed lit after the asking died." **No title card** — the UI never names him until the player writes his name.
**Look**: warden's oilcloth and tin-tagged snare belt (garment mode: workwear conversion + stripped insignia); lantern left hand, felling axe right; face restrained, half-shadowed under the hood; phase 2 face-fade advances (the plates' silhouette law, not gore).
**Arena**: 18m shallow-bowl old-growth clearing — pine-trunk colonnade (frontal staging by geometry), light shafts as emblem light. Ringed by his snare line strung with tin tags: a Living Boundary and Beautiful Hazard — contact roots you 45t and the tags chime (audio tell that you're cornered).
**Fight**: Phase 1 deliberate lantern+axe arcs (moveset in TUNING_V0.md), every move except the lantern swing carrying ≥22t punish. At ~55%: **the ceremony** — he steps back, hangs the lantern on the snare line, and self-administers E-7. 90 ticks, invulnerable, deals no damage; the arena holds its breath. Phase 2: two-handed, faster, steadier — the E-7 works, that is the horror — speech narrows further; "the quiet" Wither pulse; lantern-fire arc only punishes ring-hugging.
**Aftermath** ⚓-spirit: his belt tag carries a name. At the body, the player may write it into the notebook: grants Recollection (Names), cleanses accrued Turn, and lights the arena Hearth. Mercy as a verb after the kill — the boss beat the genre doesn't have. If the player walks away without writing, the tag stays in inventory and the border ornament shows the unwritten mark until they do.
